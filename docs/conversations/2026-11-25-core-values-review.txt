⏺ The conflict is real, because the two options mean different things by
  "retract":

  - (a) is erasing: "act as if I never said it." It protects the person who
    spoke in haste. It costs the group: replies lose their context, and someone
    can say something hurtful, watch it land, then remove the evidence.
  - (b) is withdrawing: "I said it, and I no longer stand by it." It protects
    the shared record. It costs the author, whose mistake stays on display.

  shared context is not rewritten. Both point at withdrawing. That changes my
  recommendation to a middle option:

  - (d) Folded away, not erased. A retracted comment collapses to a line saying
